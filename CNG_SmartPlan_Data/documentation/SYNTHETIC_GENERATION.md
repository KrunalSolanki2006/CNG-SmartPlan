# FUELCAST — Synthetic Data Generation Specification

**Generator:** `CNG_SmartPlan_Data/synthetic/generate_gujarat_synthetic.py`  
**Config:** `CNG_SmartPlan_Data/synthetic/synthetic_generation_config.json`  
**Version:** `2.0.0` | **Random Seed:** `20261125`

## 1. Scope & Record Counts

- **Stations:** `151` eligible verified Gujarat stations (`reference/gujarat_verified_stations.csv`)
- **Date Range:** `2024-01-01` through `2026-11-24` (`1,059` consecutive days)
- **Total Records:** `151 × 1,059 = 159,909` rows in both:
  - `synthetic/synthetic_daily_demand_sales.csv`
  - `synthetic/synthetic_supply_outages.csv`

## 2. Modeling Components (Non-IID Time Series)

1. **Station-Specific Base Demand:** Drawn within configurable profile ranges (`urban`, `highway`, `industrial`, `suburban`, `general`).
2. **Weekly Seasonality:** Profile-specific 7-day multipliers (e.g., stronger Sunday dip for `industrial`, stronger weekend retention for `highway`).
3. **Annual Seasonality & Growth Drift:** Smooth cosine annual cycle (`365.25`-day period) with station-specific phase shift and annual growth drift (`4.0%`–`5.5%/yr`).
4. **Real Calendar Effects:** Joined to `external/gujarat_holidays_festivals_2024_2026.csv`.
5. **Real Weather Effects:** Joined to `external/gujarat_district_weather_daily.csv` (rainfall dampening and ambient temperature AC-load adjustment).
6. **Temporal Autocorrelation:** AR(1) latent demand factor ($\rho = 0.72$) plus daily noise so consecutive days exhibit realistic persistence.

## 3. Separation of Demand, Supply, Outages, and Sales

- `simulated_demand_kg`: Unconstrained customer demand generated by the process above.
- `simulated_available_supply_kg`: Planned station gas supply (nominal buffer `1.06×–1.24×`, or constrained `0.78×–0.96×` on ~10% of station-days).
- `compressor_outage_flag` & `outage_capacity_factor`: Simulated compressor/maintenance events (~1.5% of station-days, reducing capacity to `0.40–0.75`).
- `effective_supply_kg = simulated_available_supply_kg * outage_capacity_factor`
- `simulated_sales_kg = min(simulated_demand_kg, effective_supply_kg)`
- `unmet_demand_kg = max(0, simulated_demand_kg - simulated_sales_kg)`

Observed generation summary (`seed=20261125`): `2,400` simulated compressor outage station-days and `18,244` supply-constrained station-days (`simulated_sales_kg < simulated_demand_kg`).
